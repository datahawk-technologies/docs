---
title: My finance data looks stale in Power BI
description: How to troubleshoot when profit or order data in Power BI stops updating or recent days look too low. Usually an unrefreshed report or data that is not due yet, not a sync issue.
---

Your Power BI report on DataHawk finance data looks frozen, or the most recent days look too low compared with the DataHawk app. In most cases the report has not refreshed, or the data for those days is not due yet. A real refresh issue on DataHawk's side is rare.

## What I can observe

- A Power BI report on `FINANCE_PROFIT`, `FINANCE_PROFIT_METRICS` or `FINANCE_ORDERS_SUCCESS` shows no rows past a certain date
- The "stuck" date can be days or weeks old
- Recent days show lower sales, fees or profit than you expect

## Why is this happening?

1. **The report has not refreshed.** This is the most common cause.
2. **The data for those days is not due yet.** Finance data for a day arrives the following day, and Amazon keeps posting fees and refunds for a few weeks after that, so the most recent days fill in over time. See [How fresh are the numbers?](/help-center/data-reference/amz-seller-profit-data#how-fresh-are-the-numbers) and [Source Systems & Freshness](/help-center/data-reference/source-systems-freshness).
3. **Less often, a query filters on a load column.** `SYNC_TIME` and `_SYNC_TIME` record when DataHawk loaded a row, not the business date, so filtering on them can hide rows.

## What can I do?

<Steps>

<Step>
### Confirm Power BI has actually refreshed

Open the Power BI report and check the "Last refresh" timestamp at the bottom-left of the workspace view, or go to **File → Options → Data Source → Refresh**.

If the last refresh is hours or days old, refresh manually (**Home → Refresh**) and recheck before going further. A surprising number of "stale data" reports come down to an unrefreshed Power BI file.
</Step>

<Step>
### Check whether the missing days are due yet

Compare the dates you are missing with the expected delays in [Source Systems & Freshness](/help-center/data-reference/source-systems-freshness). If they are within that delay, wait for the next DataHawk refresh.
</Step>

<Step>
### Check the date filters

If a query or an incremental refresh policy filters on `SYNC_TIME` or `_SYNC_TIME`, switch it to the business date: `DATE_DAY` for `FINANCE_PROFIT` and `FINANCE_PROFIT_METRICS`, `PURCHASE_DATE` for `FINANCE_ORDERS_SUCCESS`.
</Step>

<Step>
### Query the DataHawk database directly to verify the data is there

To rule out a real sync problem, run this query in <Term>Snowflake</Term> (or <Term>BigQuery</Term>) against your DataHawk database:

```sql
SELECT date_day, SUM(sales) AS sales, SUM(net_profit) AS net_profit
FROM finance.finance_profit_metrics
WHERE date_day > CURRENT_DATE() - 30
GROUP BY date_day
ORDER BY date_day;
```

If this query returns recent rows that Power BI does not show, the issue is in the report (go back to the steps above). If the query itself returns no recent rows, contact support: that is a real sync problem.
</Step>

</Steps>

## Still not working?

If you've confirmed all of:

- Power BI has refreshed recently
- The missing days are past their expected delay
- The direct warehouse query returns no recent rows

…then it's a real sync issue. Email [support@datahawk.co](mailto:support@datahawk.co) with:

- Your <Term>WSID</Term>
- The table and the date range you expected data for
- A screenshot of the warehouse query result
