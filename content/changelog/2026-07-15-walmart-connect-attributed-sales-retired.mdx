Walmart deprecated several Walmart Connect snapshot report metrics on **July 15, 2026**. From that date, these fields are empty in new data.

## What changes

**Retired fields** (empty from July 15):

- `brandAttributedSales3days`, `brandAttributedSales14days`, `brandAttributedSales30days`
- `directAttributedSales3days`, `directAttributedSales14days`, `directAttributedSales30days`
- `relatedAttributedSales3days`, `relatedAttributedSales14days`, `relatedAttributedSales30days`

**Replacement fields** (recommended by Walmart, already in your data):

- `advertisedSkuSales3days`, `advertisedSkuSales14days`, `advertisedSkuSales30days`
- `otherSkuSales3days`, `otherSkuSales14days`, `otherSkuSales30days`

This affects campaign, ad group, item, keyword, brand, category, placement, platform, page type, and video reports.

## What to do

- Update any query or dashboard that uses the retired fields.
- Data collected before July 15 doesn't change, so your history stays intact.

See [Walmart Advertising Data](/help-center/data-reference/wmt-advertising-data) for the full table reference.
