For **US, DE, UK, FR, IT, and ES**, Amazon leaves the 365+ day aging fields empty. It splits that bucket into **366 to 455 days** and **456+ days** instead. You can now see those buckets in DataHawk.

## What changes

We added six fields to `SELLER_INVENTORY_FBA_PLANNING`:

| Field | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `inv_age_366_to_455_days` | Units aged 366 to 455 days |
| `inv_age_456_plus_days` | Units aged 456+ days |
| `quantity_to_be_charged_ais_366_455_days` | Units that will incur the aged-inventory surcharge, 366 to 455 days |
| `quantity_to_be_charged_ais_456_plus_days` | Units that will incur the aged-inventory surcharge, 456+ days |
| `estimated_ais_366_455_days` | Estimated surcharge, 366 to 455 days |
| `estimated_ais_456_plus_days` | Estimated surcharge, 456+ days |

## What to do

- **US, DE, UK, FR, IT, or ES:** switch your aged-inventory reports and queries to the new fields.
- **All other marketplaces:** keep using the 365+ fields. Nothing changes for you.

See [Seller Inventory FBA Planning](/help-center/data-reference/fba-seller-inventory-fba-planning) for details.
