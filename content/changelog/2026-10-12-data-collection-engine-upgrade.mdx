Smarter prioritization, automatic recovery, and dataset-tailored scheduling strengthen our infrastructure, paving the way for upcoming agentic workflows, advanced automations, and self-driving features.

## What changes

- **Smarter prioritization:** time-sensitive data is collected first, historical refreshes run in the background.
- **Automatic recovery:** failed reports are detected and retried automatically.
- **Dataset-tailored scheduling:** each dataset is collected on the schedule that fits it, down to the marketplace's local time zone.
- **Scalability:** the new engine handles growing data volumes without slowing down collection.

## Coverage

- Amazon: catalog, pricing, finances, Seller and Vendor reports, inventory, traffic.
- Walmart: Marketplace and Connect.
- Amazon Advertising: moving to the new engine in the coming days.

## What to expect

- No action required on your side.
- Refresh times may shift slightly per marketplace.
