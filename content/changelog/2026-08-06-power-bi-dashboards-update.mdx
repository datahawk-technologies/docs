New versions of two Power BI templates are available. We released both on August 3 and shipped a follow-up fix on August 6.

## Product Dashboard

Products that Amazon marks as redirected in `PRODUCT_DETAILED` used to drop out of the dashboard. They're now included, using sales estimates and catalog data instead. Your product counts and totals are more complete as a result.

See the [Product Dashboard](/help-center/dashboard-templates/power-bi/product-dashboard) page for details.

## Seller + Vendor Analytics Dashboard

- **Exact date range:** pick a start and end date instead of "last X months".
- **Prior-year weekly figures:** calculations are now correct.
- **Prior-year dates when filtering by ASIN:** some dates went missing. Fixed on August 6.
- **Matrix:** small tweaks to the metrics shown.

See the [Seller + Vendor Analytics Dashboard](/help-center/dashboard-templates/power-bi/seller-vendor-analytics-dashboard) page for details.

## What to do

- Download the latest versions from the [Power BI templates library](/help-center/connect-tools/powerbi-templates-library).
- **Product Dashboard:** expect product counts and totals to rise slightly if some of your products are redirected.
- **Seller + Vendor Analytics Dashboard:** if you compared periods with the old version, check your prior-year weekly figures again.
