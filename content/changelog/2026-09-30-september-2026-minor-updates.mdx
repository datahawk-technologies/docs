Smaller improvements and fixes shipped in September.

## Faster CSV upload with clearer errors (September 1)

Following [last month's rebuild](/changelog/2026-08-06-csv-upload-rebuilt), the CSV upload for bulk operations got three upgrades:

- **Faster:** large files upload more quickly.
- **Clearer errors:** you see what's wrong and where.
- **Flexible dates:** we now normalize date formats on upload.

## Amazon Italy: more complete product attributes (September 21)

We fixed a collection issue that left some product attributes incomplete for Amazon-IT. No action needed.
