title: "Amazon DSP: correct account names"
description: "DSP accounts now show their own advertiser account name instead of the Manager Account name in AMZN_DSP_CAMPAIGNS."
date: "2026-08-27"
tags: ["fix"]
---

For DSP accounts, `seller_name` in `ADVERTISING.AMZN_DSP_CAMPAIGNS` showed the Manager Account name instead of the DSP advertiser account name. Separate DSP accounts could look like duplicates.

## What changes

Each DSP advertiser ID now maps to its own account name.

## What to expect

- No action required on your side.
- If you grouped or filtered by `seller_name`, your DSP accounts now appear under their own names.
