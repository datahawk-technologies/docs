On Amazon, one category can sit under several parent paths. For example, category X can be reached through `A > B > X` and `A > C > X`.

## What changes

Category tracking now relies on each category's unique ID, not the path you took to reach it.

- **No duplicates:** track X from two different paths and you get one tracked category, not two.
- **Faster:** category data processes more quickly.

## What to expect

- No action required on your side.
- If you tracked the same category through several paths, it now appears once in your tracked categories.

See [Categories tracking](/help-center/data-setup/categories-tracking) for how tracking works.
