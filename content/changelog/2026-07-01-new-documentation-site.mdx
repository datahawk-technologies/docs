As of **July 1, 2026**, all DataHawk help content lives on a new documentation site.

## What changes

- **Clearer structure:** content is grouped into Welcome, Help Center, Troubleshooting, and Changelog.
- **Site-wide search:** find any page, table, or metric from the search bar.
- **Feedback on every page:** tell us if a page helped, or what's missing, using the widget at the bottom.

## What to expect

- No action required on your side.
