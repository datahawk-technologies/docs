---
title: Set up DataHawk MCP
description: How to connect DataHawk MCP to AI clients like Claude, ChatGPT, n8n, Make, and more.
---

Setting up DataHawk MCP means adding one link, the DataHawk MCP endpoint, to your AI tool, then signing in with your DataHawk account. Once connected, your AI can answer questions about your Amazon and Walmart data. It works with chat assistants, code editors, and automation platforms.

<Callout type="info">
  **Not sure which to pick?** Most people start with **Claude** or **ChatGPT**. They're the fastest to set up (under 2 minutes) and the best fit for everyday questions. Use a code editor or app builder if you're building apps, or n8n or Make to schedule automations.
</Callout>

## Before you start

<Steps>

<Step>
### Check that your plan includes MCP

DataHawk MCP is in beta. It's enabled by default on active DataHawk workspaces. Not sure your plan includes it? Ask your Account Manager or email [support@datahawk.co](mailto:support@datahawk.co).
</Step>

<Step>
### Copy the DataHawk MCP endpoint

You'll paste this link into your AI tool in the next section:

```
https://mcp.datahawk.co/mcp
```
</Step>

<Step>
### Check your data sources

MCP only returns data from the accounts you've connected in DataHawk. Before you start, check that these are connected and syncing on the Sources page:

- **Amazon:** your Seller Central or Vendor Central account, your Advertising account, and (optional) DSP.
- **Walmart:** your Walmart Marketplace and Walmart Connect accounts.

Fix anything marked as disconnected first. Otherwise MCP returns empty results for that source. MCP doesn't cover every Walmart dataset yet: see [Walmart coverage](/help-center/modules/datahawkmcp#walmart-coverage).
</Step>

</Steps>

## Connect your AI tool

### Chat with your data

<Tabs items={['Claude', 'ChatGPT']}>

<Tab value="Claude">

<Callout type="info">
  For more detail, see Anthropic's [custom connector guide](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11175166-get-started-with-custom-connectors-using-remote-mcp).
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn">
  On a Claude Team or Enterprise plan, your Claude admin adds the connector for your organization.
</Callout>

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open your connectors

In Claude, go to **Customize → Connectors**.

<img
  src="/images/help-center/modules/datahawkmcp/setup/MCP-Claude-1.png"
  alt="Claude menu with the Customize option"
  width={300}
  height={170}
  className="rounded-lg border"
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Add a custom connector

Click **Add custom connector**.

<img
  src="/images/help-center/modules/datahawkmcp/setup/MCP-Claude-3.png"
  alt="Add custom connector button in Claude"
  width={600}
  height={340}
  className="rounded-lg border"
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Name it and paste the endpoint

Name the connector `DataHawk` and paste the endpoint:

```
https://mcp.datahawk.co/mcp
```

<img
  src="/images/help-center/modules/datahawkmcp/setup/MCP-Claude-4.png"
  alt="Custom connector form with the DataHawk name and endpoint filled in"
  width={600}
  height={340}
  className="rounded-lg border"
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Connect

Click **Connect**.

<img
  src="/images/help-center/modules/datahawkmcp/setup/MCP-Claude-5.png"
  alt="Connect button next to the DataHawk connector"
  width={600}
  height={340}
  className="rounded-lg border"
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Sign in to DataHawk

Claude sends you to DataHawk. Sign in with your DataHawk account.

<img
  src="/images/help-center/modules/datahawkmcp/setup/MCP-Claude-6.png"
  alt="DataHawk sign-in page"
  width={300}
  height={170}
  className="rounded-lg border"
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Approve the permissions

Approve the permissions screen. DataHawk now shows in your connectors list with a green status.
</Step>

</Steps>

</Tab>

<Tab value="ChatGPT">

<Callout type="info">
  For more detail, see OpenAI's [apps in ChatGPT guide](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11487775-apps-in-chatgpt).
</Callout>

<Steps>

<Step>
### Open the Apps settings

In ChatGPT, click your profile, then **Settings → Apps**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Open the custom MCP server option

Click **Advanced settings → Custom MCP server**.

<img
  src="/images/help-center/modules/datahawkmcp/setup/MCP-ChatGPT-1.png"
  alt="Advanced settings in ChatGPT Apps, with the Custom MCP server option"
  width={600}
  height={340}
  className="rounded-lg border"
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Turn on developer mode

Turn on **Developer mode**.

<img
  src="/images/help-center/modules/datahawkmcp/setup/MCP-ChatGPT-2.png"
  alt="Developer mode toggle in ChatGPT"
  width={600}
  height={340}
  className="rounded-lg border"
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Create the app

Click **Create app** and fill in the form:

- **Name:** `DataHawk`
- **MCP Server URL:** `https://mcp.datahawk.co/mcp`

<img
  src="/images/help-center/modules/datahawkmcp/setup/MCP-ChatGPT-3.png"
  alt="Create app form in ChatGPT"
  width={300}
  height={170}
  className="rounded-lg border"
/>
</Step>

<Step>
### Confirm

Tick **I understand and want to continue**, then click **Create**. Sign in with your DataHawk account when asked.
</Step>

<Step>
### Check it's connected

DataHawk now shows in your enabled apps with a green status.

<img
  src="/images/help-center/modules/datahawkmcp/setup/MCP-ChatGPT-4.png"
  alt="DataHawk listed in ChatGPT's enabled apps"
  width={600}
  height={430}
  className="rounded-lg border"
/>
</Step>

</Steps>
