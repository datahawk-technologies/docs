Category tracking is how you monitor the competitive landscape around your own products: which competitors rank where, how those rankings shift over time, and what categories are heating up. For each tracked category, DataHawk keeps a daily snapshot of the top 100 products with their sales rank, price, rating, and key metadata.

<Callout type="warn">
  Only available for Amazon
</Callout>

## Category tracking capabilities

### What data does DataHawk collect?

For each category you track, DataHawk will provide you with a daily snapshot of these primary product attributes:

- category name
- category path
- top 100 products
- bestseller rank
- product price
- title
- star rating
- numbers of ratings, including reviews
- A+ status
- product image URL
- estimated sales

### Supported regions

Category tracking works in all Amazon regions:

- **North America**: US, Canada, Mexico, and Brazil.
- **Europe**: UK, Germany, Italy, France, Spain, Netherlands, UAE, Sweden, India, and Poland.
- **Far East**: Japan, Singapore, and Australia.

<Callout type="info">
  Category tracking is country-specific. If you track the same category in multiple marketplaces, each <Term>marketplace</Term> is counted as a separate tracked category.
</Callout>

### History

When you add a category, you also get up to 2 years of its past data. From then on, DataHawk adds new data every day and keeps everything: after one year of tracking, you have up to 3 years of data; after two years, up to 4; and so on.

It may take up to 24 hours for the new category and its history to show up. After that, DataHawk keeps collecting the category every day.

### Automated data sync and backup

We then **securely store and back up** all your data **every day**, enabling **more comprehensive reporting with extended historical context**.

### Refresh Frequency

Data is updated daily. Data from the last 24 hours may be delayed.
