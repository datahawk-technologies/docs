---
title: Amazon Vendor Demand Forecasts
description: How VENDOR_FORECASTING_SRC shows Amazon's latest weekly demand forecast, and how to get past forecasts to compare with your sales.
---

Every week, Amazon publishes a new demand forecast for each of your <Term>ASIN</Term>s in Vendor Central. Each forecast covers the next 48 weeks. DataHawk collects it and delivers it to `SELLING_PARTNER.VENDOR_FORECASTING_SRC` every Wednesday. This page explains what the table shows and how to compare a past forecast with your sales.

## What's in the table

The table holds one row per ASIN and forecasted week. Each row carries:

- **Generation date:** the day Amazon produced the forecast.
- **Start date:** the first day of the forecasted week.
- **Forecasted units:** Amazon's Mean, P70, P80, and P90 demand forecasts for that week. In short, the higher the P-level, the more cautious the number.

DataHawk serves these values as Amazon sends them, without recalculating them. For Amazon's exact definitions and report rules, see [Amazon's Vendor Forecasting Report documentation](https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/docs/report-type-values-analytics#vendor-forecasting-report).

## Only the latest forecast is kept

The table always shows **Amazon's latest forecast**. Each week, the new forecast replaces the previous one entirely. Older forecasts don't stay in the table.

Here's how it works week by week:

| Week | DataHawk collects | What the table shows |
|---|---|---|
| **W0** | Forecast for W0 to W48 | Forecast for W0 to W48 |
| **W1** | Forecast for W1 to W49 | Forecast for W1 to W49. The W0 forecast is gone |
| **W2** | Forecast for W2 to W50 | Forecast for W2 to W50. The W1 forecast is gone |

And so on, every week.

## Compare a past forecast with your sales

Want to check how accurate Amazon's forecast was? You'll need the forecast as it looked on a given date, then compare it with your actual sales as the weeks go by. You have two ways to get it.

### Option 1: save the forecast yourself

When you want to track a forecast, save a copy of it after Wednesday's delivery, before the next one replaces it. For example:

- Download the Forecasting report from Vendor Central.
- Export the table from your DataHawk destination (Snowflake, BigQuery, or another tool).

Later, compare that saved copy with your sales data. Plan ahead: once a newer forecast is out, neither Amazon nor the table will show the old one.

### Option 2: ask DataHawk

Amazon only shares its most recent forecast. DataHawk, however, keeps every past forecast on its side. Forgot to save one? We can send it to you.

## Frequently asked questions

<Accordions>

<Accordion title="Why can't I see older forecasts in the table?">

The table only keeps Amazon's latest forecast. Each new weekly forecast replaces the previous one. See [Only the latest forecast is kept](#only-the-latest-forecast-is-kept).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I get Amazon's forecast from several months ago?">

Yes. Amazon doesn't share old forecasts, but DataHawk stores them and can send them to you. See [Option 2: ask DataHawk](#option-2-ask-datahawk).

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How often does the table refresh?">

Every Wednesday, not daily like other Vendor data. DataHawk collects and delivers Amazon's newest forecast that day. For Amazon's publishing schedule, see [Amazon's documentation](https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/docs/report-type-values-analytics#vendor-forecasting-report). See [Source Systems & Freshness](/help-center/data-reference/source-systems-freshness).

</Accordion>

</Accordions>
