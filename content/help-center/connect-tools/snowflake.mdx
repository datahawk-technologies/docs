Snowflake is a cloud-native data warehouse built for performance and scale. DataHawk uses Snowflake as the hosted database for users who work with Power BI, Excel, Tableau, or any SQL-compatible tool outside of Google's ecosystem.

## Your DataHawk Snowflake database

When you set up a Snowflake destination, DataHawk creates and manages a dedicated database containing all your collected data. You don't need to configure or maintain anything. We handle setup, scaling, and maintenance.

What's included:

- Daily updated tables, fresh and analysis-ready
- Optimized views, cleaned and enriched by DataHawk
- No infrastructure management on your side

DataHawk refreshes your Snowflake database every day. Not all data arrives at the same time. Some datasets are populated later in the day depending on when Amazon's API makes them available. See the individual dataset pages in the Knowledge Hub for dataset-specific freshness details.

## Option 1: DataHawk-hosted Snowflake (recommended for most users)

This is the default setup. DataHawk creates and hosts the Snowflake database for you.

### How to find your credentials

In your DataHawk <Term>workspace</Term>, navigate to **Setup \> Destinations \> Snowflake \> See Credentials**.

Your credentials page will contain the following:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| **User Name** | Your Snowflake username |
| **Public Key** | The public part of your key pair. Generate it here and keep the downloaded private-key file and passphrase for your tool. |
| **Server** | The Snowflake account URL (e.g. `xyz123.eu-west-1.snowflakecomputing.com`) |
| **Database** | The name of your DataHawk database |
| **Warehouse** | The compute warehouse to use for queries |
| **Role** | The access role to use for your connection |

Keep these credentials safe. You'll use them to connect your database to your BI tool or SQL editor.

## Switch from a password to a key pair

Snowflake is ending password-based connections for tools and dashboards. To keep them connected to your data, DataHawk now lets you use a key pair instead of a password.

A **password** is a secret you enter to connect. A **key pair** has two matching parts. DataHawk sends the public part to Snowflake, and you download the private part as a file for your tool to use. A **passphrase** protects that private file, like a password protecting a document. Your tool uses the file and passphrase together instead of your current password.

### What are the impacts?

From **January 31, 2027**, tools and dashboards still using a **Password** to connect to your DataHawk Snowflake account will no longer connect or refresh. Update them before this date. Your data and access permissions stay the same.

### What should I do?

Ask an Admin of your DataHawk workspace to:

1. Open **Setup > Destinations > Snowflake > [See Credentials](https://analytics.datahawk.co/app/destinations/snowflake-ecosystem)** and choose the connection used by your tool.
2. Under **Public Key**, click **Generate keypair 1**.
3. Choose and confirm a passphrase of at least eight characters, then click **Generate and download**.
4. Keep the downloaded private-key file and its passphrase somewhere safe. You cannot download the same private key again.

<Callout type="warn">
If a key already exists, check with your team before regenerating it. Replacing it stops connections that use the previous private key from working once the new key is active.
</Callout>

### Use the key pair in your tool or dashboard

Open your tool's Snowflake connection settings. Choose **key-pair authentication**, then provide the downloaded private-key file and its passphrase. Keep your other connection settings unchanged.

Update every tool and dashboard using this connection, including automatic refreshes. Test that each one connects and refreshes successfully. Generating a key in DataHawk does not update your tools automatically, and the new key may take a little time to become active.

### If the private key or passphrase is lost

Ask your DataHawk workspace Admin to generate a replacement, then update your connected tools with the new file and passphrase. The original download cannot be recovered through DataHawk.

### Problems?

If you have connectivity issues or need assistance, feel free to contact support. Do not send your private key or passphrase.

## Option 2: Self-hosted Snowflake (advanced)

If you already use Snowflake and prefer to have DataHawk write data into your own Snowflake account, this is possible.

To get started, contact your Account Manager and share your:

- [Snowflake account identifier](https://docs.snowflake.com/en/user-guide/admin-account-identifier)
- Cloud hosting provider (AWS, Azure, GCP) and region

## Snowflake Credits

DataHawk-hosted Snowflake databases consume Snowflake credits. Credits are used when your warehouse is active (running queries).

See [Snowflake credit usage and consumption monitoring](/help-center/data-reference/snowflake-credit-monitoring) to understand how credits are consumed, what happens if you exceed your quota, and how to track your own usage with self-serve SQL queries.

## Query timeout (30 minutes)

<Callout type="warn">
Since **November 2025**, all Snowflake Data Sharing queries are subject to a **default 30-minute timeout**. Queries that exceed 30 minutes are cancelled by the warehouse. This guardrail prevents runaway queries from consuming credits and is applied at the warehouse level for every customer.
</Callout>

**How to verify the limit:** In your SQL client, run `SHOW PARAMETERS LIKE 'STATEMENT_TIMEOUT_IN_SECONDS' IN WAREHOUSE <your_warehouse>;`: The value should be `1800` (seconds).

**Write queries that finish in under 30 minutes:**

- Filter early: Restrict by `marketplace_key`, `date`, brand, or ASIN before joining wide tables.
- Limit date ranges in exploratory queries (e.g. `WHERE date >= DATEADD(month, -3, CURRENT_DATE)`).
- Use the curated `REPORTS.*` views when possible. They are pre-aggregated and substantially faster than building from raw layers.
- Avoid `SELECT *` on the largest raw tables (advertising, profit ledger, search). Project only the columns you need.
- Break very large extracts into multiple smaller queries (e.g. month-by-month) rather than a single multi-year scan.

**If a query is cancelled by the timeout:**

- Check the Snowflake query history (`QUERY_HISTORY`) to confirm the cancellation reason.
- See [Accessing our Snowflake database from behind a firewall](/troubleshooting/connection-failures/accessing-our-snowflake-database-from-behind-a-firewall) for network-side issues that can also cause long-running or stalled queries.

## Connect an AI assistant

You can connect Claude, ChatGPT, or another AI assistant straight to your DataHawk Snowflake database with Snowflake MCP, Snowflake's native MCP server. Your AI then runs read-only queries on every table, using your Snowflake credits. See [Use AI with your DataHawk data](/help-center/connect-tools/ai-assistants#snowflake-mcp).

## Compatible tools

Snowflake connects natively to most modern data tools. Common examples:
