**Data update - Resolved**

**Issue type:** Partial

**Period:** Nov 18, 2026

**Scope:** Walmart: about 40% of tracked keywords have no data. Amazon: most keywords have data, but not all 5 result pages, so lower positions are missing. Data from Nov 19 is complete.

**Datasets:**

- `SEO.SEO_PRODUCT_ORGANIC_RANK`
- `SEO.SEO_PRODUCT_SPONSORED_RANK`

**Cause:** An internal processing issue affected updates. Search results weren't fully collected that day, so there's nothing to rebuild the missing ranks from.

**Progress:** Resolved.

**Historical data:** Some data for Nov 18, 2026 could not be recovered; these gaps are permanent.

**Action:** Exclude Nov 18 from rank trends. A product missing from Amazon results that day may have dropped below the collected pages rather than out of search.
