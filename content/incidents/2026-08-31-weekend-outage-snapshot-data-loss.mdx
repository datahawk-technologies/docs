Over the weekend of August 30 and 31, DataHawk had a data collection outage. A deployment went out missing one of its configuration files, which caused the jobs that collect data to fail repeatedly through the weekend. We picked this up on the morning of August 31 and rolled the change back the same morning. Collection is back to normal now, but snapshot-based data from that weekend could not be recovered.

## What this means for you

Snapshot-based datasets capture a single point-in-time view of your data. When collection stops, that snapshot is skipped, and there is no way to reconstruct it after the fact. This is different from data that gets refreshed on the next run - here, the gap stays in your history.

The Raw Inventory (FBA) dataset was affected. If your reports or dashboards pull FBA inventory data for August 30 or 31, expect a gap for those two days.

## What we are doing about it

We rolled back the deployment and restarted collection the same morning, and everything collected since is complete. The gap itself cannot be backfilled.

If a specific report or query depends on that date range, reach out to your account team and we will help you work around the gap.
