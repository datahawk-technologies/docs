Several North America datasets arrived late or incomplete for data dates between
October 9 and October 12. We identified the delay on October 13 and are
reloading the missing data now. Other regions weren't affected.

## What this means for you

If you query North America data, you may see these gaps:

- **Advertising, Oct 12:** `ADVERTISING.ADVERTISING_CAMPAIGN_METRICS` and
  `ADVERTISING.ADVERTISING_PRODUCT_METRICS` had no data.
- **Retail traffic, Oct 11:** `SELLING_PARTNER.SELLER_RETAIL_TRAFFIC_BY_ASIN_SRC`
  had no data.
- **Vendor reports, Oct 9:** the four vendor sales and inventory tables
  (manufacturing and sourcing views) hold only a small share of the usual rows.
- **FBA planning and restock, Oct 12:** `SELLING_PARTNER.SELLER_INVENTORY_FBA_PLANNING`
  and `RAW_INVENTORY.RAW_INVENTORY_RESTOCK_RECOMMENDATIONS_HISTORY` are missing
  about 12 to 14% of their usual rows.

## What we are doing about it

We're reloading the missing data, and advertising data has already started to
catch up. We'll update this entry once every table is complete. Until then,
treat these dates as incomplete. If you exported them, pull them again after we
mark this incident resolved.
