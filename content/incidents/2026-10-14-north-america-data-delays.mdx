Several North America datasets arrived late or incomplete for data dates between
October 9 and October 12. We identified the delay on October 13 at 17:00 CEST
and reloaded all missing data by 19:00 CEST the same day. Other regions weren't
affected, and no data was lost.

## What this means for you

If you queried North America data before 19:00 CEST on October 13, you may have
seen these gaps:

- **Advertising, Oct 12:** `ADVERTISING.ADVERTISING_CAMPAIGN_METRICS` and
  `ADVERTISING.ADVERTISING_PRODUCT_METRICS` had no data.
- **Retail traffic, Oct 11:** `SELLING_PARTNER.SELLER_RETAIL_TRAFFIC_BY_ASIN_SRC`
  had no data.
- **Vendor reports, Oct 9:** the four vendor sales and inventory tables
  (manufacturing and sourcing views) held only a small share of the usual rows.
- **FBA planning and restock, Oct 12:** `SELLING_PARTNER.SELLER_INVENTORY_FBA_PLANNING`
  and `RAW_INVENTORY.RAW_INVENTORY_RESTOCK_RECOMMENDATIONS_HISTORY` were missing
  about 12 to 14% of their usual rows.

## What we are doing about it

All affected tables are now complete for these dates. If you exported any of
them before 19:00 CEST on October 13, pull them again. Otherwise, no action is
needed: your history is complete.
