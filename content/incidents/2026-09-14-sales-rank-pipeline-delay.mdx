On September 14, we noticed Sales Rank and Best Seller Rank data had stopped updating since September 11. The cause was a processing job that failed to start correctly, which meant new data was not being produced for those datasets.

## What this means for you

If you checked either dataset between September 12 and 14, you would have seen no new data for that stretch. No data was lost - it just had not been produced yet.

## What we are doing about it

We fixed the job the same day. Processing completed and the missing days flowed through on the next pipeline run, so your history for this period is now complete.
