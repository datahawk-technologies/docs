FBA inventory and planning data for September 20, 2026 came in with about half
the usual number of products. We spotted it on September 21 and recovered the
missing records on September 22. September 21 and 22 were complete throughout.

Update, September 22: the missing September 20 data has been recovered and
reloaded. Nothing was lost.

## What this means for you

September 20 was complete. If you queried Raw Inventory (FBA), Seller
Inventory FBA History Daily, or Seller Inventory FBA Planning for that date
before the recovery, you may have seen a gap in product coverage, most visibly in
North America. The figures that were present were correct, so this was missing
products rather than wrong numbers.

## What we are doing about it

Amazon's daily inventory reports for September 20 reached us late and incomplete,
so the snapshot we stored for that day was short. We have since retrieved the
full reports and reloaded them. If you pulled September 20 before the recovery,
run your query again and you will get complete coverage.
