FBA inventory and planning data for September 20, 2026 arrived with roughly half
the usual number of products. We identified this on September 21. September 21
and 22 are complete. September 20 is still under investigation.

The shortfall has two sources: a change to our North America collection schedule,
and incomplete planning reports returned by Amazon.

## What this means for you

Some of your products have no rows for September 20. The values that are there
are correct, so this shows up as missing product coverage, not as wrong numbers.
Whole accounts and marketplaces are unaffected, and North America is the most
affected region.

## What we are doing about it

We have adjusted the North America collection schedule, and are working to
recover September 20 and confirm which accounts and marketplaces were affected.
We will update this entry as the investigation progresses. Until then, treat the
September 20 drop as incomplete data, not a real change in your catalog.
