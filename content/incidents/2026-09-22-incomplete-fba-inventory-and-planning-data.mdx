FBA inventory and planning data for September 20, 2026 came in with about half
the usual number of products. We spotted it on September 21. September 21 and 22
are complete. September 20 is still being investigated.

## What this means for you

Some of your products are missing for September 20. The figures that are there
are correct, so you will see a gap in product coverage rather than wrong numbers.
North America is the most affected region.

## What we are doing about it

We are working to recover September 20. We will update this entry as we learn more. Until then,
treat the September 20 gap as missing data, not a real change in your catalog.
