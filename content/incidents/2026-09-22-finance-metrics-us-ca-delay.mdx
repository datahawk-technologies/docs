On the morning of September 22, finance product metrics for September 21 were
incomplete: a large share of rows for the US and Canada marketplaces had not yet
arrived. Data collection had been running less frequently than usual, so part of
that day's data came in later than normal.

## What this means for you

If you queried `FINANCE.FINANCE_PRODUCT_METRICS_DAILY` for September 21 before
late morning (CEST) on September 22, figures for US and Canada may have looked
lower than expected or been missing. Other marketplaces and other dates were not
affected, and no data was lost. The unique product and account counts were
correct throughout; only part of the daily volume was late.

## What we are doing about it

We restored the normal collection frequency the same morning, and the missing
rows flowed through on the next run within a few hours. Your history for
September 21 is now complete. If you exported or cached that date early on
September 22, pull it again; otherwise no action is needed.
