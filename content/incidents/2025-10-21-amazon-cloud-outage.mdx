Amazon had a major cloud outage that affected some of its own systems. Because DataHawk depends on Amazon for data collection, this caused delays in data availability on our side.

## What this means for you

The issue was entirely on Amazon's end, not ours. Once Amazon resolved the outage, all delayed data was caught up the same day.

## What we are doing about it

Nothing was lost, and no action was needed beyond monitoring the catch-up. We are including this entry for completeness, since it was already communicated to customers at the time.
