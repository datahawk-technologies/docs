**Data update - Identified**

**Issue type:** Incorrect data

**Period:** From May 2026

**Scope:** Amazon products with ratings, a share of rows (most rows since May 2026). Walmart isn't affected.

**Datasets:** `PRODUCT.PRODUCT_DETAILED` (`RATING_1_STARS_PERCENT` to `RATING_5_STARS_PERCENT`)

**Cause:** An Amazon page change stopped us from reading the star-rating breakdown, and the missing values were stored as 0. `RATING` and `RATING_COUNT` were correct.

**Progress:** Fixed for new data from Oct 7, 2026. Missing breakdowns are updated and when no data comes through, it is now NULL, not 0.

**Historical data:** We're reviewing how far back we can correct it.

**Action:** We are working on restoring the historical data. If you queried this dataset for Amazon products with ratings, you may have seen 0s in the star-rating breakdown columns. 
Before Oct 7, 2026, you can treat old all-zero breakdowns as missing for now while we restore them, not 0%. From Oct 7, NULL means Amazon shows no breakdown for that product.
