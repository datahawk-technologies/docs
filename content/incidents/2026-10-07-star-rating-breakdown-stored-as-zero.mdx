**Data update - Resolved**

**Issue type:** Incorrect data

**Period:** May 2026 - Oct 6, 2026

**Scope:** Amazon products with ratings, a share of rows (most rows since May 2026). Walmart isn't affected.

**Datasets:** `PRODUCT.PRODUCT_DETAILED` (`RATING_1_STARS_PERCENT` to `RATING_5_STARS_PERCENT`)

**Cause:** An Amazon page change stopped us from reading the star-rating breakdown, and the missing values were stored as 0. `RATING` and `RATING_COUNT` were correct.

**Progress:** Resolved.

**Historical data:** All affected data has been restored or corrected.

**Action:** If you exported the star-rating breakdown columns before Oct 9, 2026, pull them again. From Oct 7, 2026, NULL means Amazon shows no breakdown for that product.

Update, Oct 9, 2026: the historical star-rating breakdowns have been corrected.
