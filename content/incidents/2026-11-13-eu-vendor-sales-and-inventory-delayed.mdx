**Data update - Resolved**

**Issue type:** Delayed

**Period:** Nov 7 - Nov 8, 2026

**Scope:** EU marketplaces. Vendor sales and inventory tables had no data for these dates until Amazon delivered it. Other regions weren't affected.

**Datasets:**

- `SELLING_PARTNER.VENDOR_SALES_MANUFACTURING_SRC`
- `SELLING_PARTNER.VENDOR_SALES_SOURCING_SRC`
- `SELLING_PARTNER.VENDOR_INVENTORY_MANUFACTURING_SRC`
- `SELLING_PARTNER.VENDOR_INVENTORY_SOURCING_SRC`

**Cause:** A confirmed issue with Amazon affected source data.

**Progress:** Resolved.

**Historical data:** All affected data has been restored or corrected.

**Action:** If you excluded Nov 7 and Nov 8 from EU vendor reporting, include them again and refresh your reports.

Update, Nov 19, 2026: Amazon delivered the Nov 7 and Nov 8 data, and no data was lost.
