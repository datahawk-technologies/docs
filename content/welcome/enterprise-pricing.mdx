This page covers Enterprise plans only. Standard and Growth have fixed monthly prices, listed on [Upgrade your DataHawk plan](/welcome/upgrade). Enterprise pricing is custom: it's built from three parts, a platform fee plus usage-based fees for <Term>Account Analytics</Term> and <Term>Digital Shelf Analytics</Term>. Use this page to get oriented before you talk plan details with your DataHawk contact.

## 1. Platform fee

A flat base fee that covers the database credits you need. The standard amount is 20 credits per month.

## 2. Account Analytics

Account Analytics covers sales, ads, listings, inventory, and day-to-day account health.

Its price depends on how much data your connected accounts produce:

- **Seller accounts:** units sold or orders per year
- **Vendor accounts:** catalog size
- **Advertising accounts:** ad spend per year

## 3. Digital Shelf Analytics

Digital Shelf Analytics is DataHawk's market and competitive intelligence layer. It shows how products look and compete in a category: best sellers, keyword ranks, new entrants, price trends, product page changes, and more.

Its price depends on how many products, keywords, and categories you track per <Term>Marketplace</Term>:

<div style={{ marginBottom: '2rem' }}>
| You track | What data you get |
|---|---|
| Product | Listing details, sales ranks, attributes, pricing, offers, <Term>Buy Box</Term>, rating, and rating count |
| Keyword | Organic ranks and SEO visibility |
| Premium Keyword | Organic ranks plus sponsored ranks, for deeper keyword visibility. See [Premium Keywords](/help-center/data-setup/premium-keywords-tracking). |
| Category | Top 100 products, market share, brand performance |
</div>

<div style={{ marginBottom: '5rem' }}>
<Callout type="info">
  Tracking a product doesn't include its keyword or SEO data. You need keyword tracking for that. Volume discounts apply based on total contract size.
</Callout>
</div>
