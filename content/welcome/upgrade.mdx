Thanks for trying DataHawk! Your free trial runs 14 days and includes one account of each type (Amazon Seller Central, Vendor Central, and Advertising; Walmart Marketplace and Connect), plus 50 tracked products, 50 organic keywords, and 5 categories. To keep going after that, pick a package. Each one sets limits on how much data you collect and who can use it. Send us an email and we'll set up your subscription for you.

<Callout type="info">
  The days left in your trial show in the navigation bar of the DataHawk app. When the trial ends, your access is reduced, but your workspace and data aren't deleted. Upgrade any time to pick up where you left off.
</Callout>

## Going Enterprise? Tell us what you need

Enterprise plans have a custom price based on how much data you collect. Add these numbers to your email, totaled across every account you plan to connect:

- **Tracking:** number of products, keywords, and categories. For keywords, say if you need organic ranks only or [Premium Keywords](/help-center/data-setup/premium-keywords-tracking) (organic + sponsored ranks).
- **Seller Central:** units sold per year
- **Vendor Central:** catalog size (products sold in the last 12 months)
- **Advertising:** ad spend per year

Want to know how these numbers shape your price? Read [Enterprise pricing](/welcome/enterprise-pricing), or try the [pricing simulator](https://pricing.datahawk.co/).
