You can try DataHawk on your own now. Signing up creates your account and your first <Term>workspace</Term>, the space where your data, sources, and teammates live. Your 14-day trial starts the moment that workspace exists.

Got an invitation from a teammate instead? Skip this page. Open the invitation email and follow [Quick Start Guide](/welcome/getting-started/quick-start) from Step 2.

## Create your account

<Steps>

<Step>

### Sign up

Go to the DataHawk sign-up page and pick one option:

- **Email:** enter your email and a password. We'll send you a verification email. Click the link inside, then click **Continue to workspace**.
- **Google:** sign in with your Google account. No separate password needed.


</Step>

<Step>

### Tell us about your company

Answer a short survey. It takes about a minute and helps us tailor your setup:

- Company name and website
- Country
- Company type: brand, seller, agency, SaaS, or other
- Yearly marketplace revenue, in USD. Agencies enter the revenue they manage for clients. Pre-revenue is a valid answer.
- What you want to do with DataHawk, such as Seller Central analytics, advertising, or Buy Box monitoring. Pick at least one.

Accept the terms of service, then submit.

</Step>

<Step>

### Land in your workspace

DataHawk creates your workspace and starts your trial. A welcome screen shows your trial dates, what's included, and links to connect your first data sources. Close it whenever you're ready. It won't show up again.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  While you explore, DataHawk prepares your database in the background. You can start connecting sources right away. Your database may take a little longer before it shows as ready for tools like Power BI.
</Callout>

## What your trial includes

Your trial lasts 14 days from the moment your workspace is created. It includes:

| | Trial limit |
|---|---|
| **Amazon Seller Central accounts** | 1 |
| **Amazon Vendor Central accounts** | 1 |
| **Amazon Advertising accounts** | 1 |
| **Walmart Marketplace accounts** | 1 |
| **Walmart Connect accounts** | 1 |
| **Tracked products** | 50 |
| **Organic keywords** | 50 |
| **Categories** | 5 |

You also get the full platform: [Alerts](/help-center/modules/alerts), [AI Copywriter](/help-center/modules/ai-copywriter), [Sherlock](/help-center/modules/sherlock), dashboard templates, and direct database access.

## Keep track of your trial

The navigation bar shows how many days you have left. Click **Upgrade** there to compare plans. Clicking it doesn't charge you or change anything in your workspace.

## Common questions

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="What happens when my trial ends?">
    You data will not be refreshed anymore, but your workspace and account aren't deleted. Upgrade at any time to get full access back. {/* TODO confirm exact post-expiry behavior and how long data is kept. */}
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I invite my teammates during the trial?">
    Yes. Invite them from **Settings → Members**. They join your workspace and share your trial. Joining doesn't restart it. See [Members Management](/help-center/datahawk-app/members-management).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I signed up twice by accident. Do I have two workspaces?">
    No. If you submit the form more than once, DataHawk keeps a single workspace.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
